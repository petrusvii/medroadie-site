Questions

Good to know

How fast can we be live?

Usually within a week. We set up your server, import your current material and categories, and invite your first users.

Do our reps need to install anything?

No. It runs in the browser on iPad, iPhone, Android and laptops. Reps can add it to the home screen so it opens like an app.

Where is our data?

On your own dedicated server, in the US or the EU, with daily backups. Files are never publicly accessible.

Can surgeons use it?

Yes, in two ways: a personal share link from their rep, or their own account that only shows material approved for surgeons in their market.

We are ISO 13485 / FDA regulated. What about validation?

MedRoadie comes with a risk-based validation plan and test evidence following FDA's Computer Software Assurance approach. We can fill it in for your setup as part of the Custom plan.
